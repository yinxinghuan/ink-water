# Attribution

Ink & Water is adapted from **“Ink and Water simulation”** by **Karim Maaloul (Yakudoo)**:

- Original: https://codepen.io/Yakudoo/pen/bGOMJPw
- Author: https://codepen.io/Yakudoo/
- Original description: a fluid simulation experiment using frame buffer objects and custom shaders.
- CodePen license link: https://blog.codepen.io/documentation/licensing/

The port preserves the original scene, shaders, FBO ping-pong simulation, parameters, brush-tip mesh, camera, lights, shadows and `noise_1.jpg` simulation texture. AlterU additions include touch input, two-finger diffusion control, click-to-start resource gating, onboarding, progress/result states, responsive presentation and audio feedback.
